The concept

A third space beyond home and work.

Opened in a former pharmacy on N Mac Dill Ave, AstroBrew fills the gap between daytime specialty coffee shops and late-night bars. Come for a cold brew at 9, return for a kombucha at 6. Bring your kids, your grandparents, anyone.

Our crew loves to explain what’s in the glass, from functional mushrooms to kava, so first-timers always feel at home.

2022

Founded by Mike Pool and Trixie Szilagyi, a locally owned café with its own wholesale arm.

      Our story

      Named after an impact crater.

      When the founders asked Siri her favorite word, she said “Astrobleme”: a crater left by a meteorite. They kept it, because they wanted to make products with a real impact.

      Philosophy

      Great drinks, plain-spoken education. Many guests come looking for an alternative to alcohol that still feels social and serene, and we think that’s a space worth designing well.

      “This is a place where you can bring your kids, your grandparents, anyone really.”
